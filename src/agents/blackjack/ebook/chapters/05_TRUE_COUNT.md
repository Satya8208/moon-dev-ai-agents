# Chapter 5: True Count Mastery

## Why True Count is Everything

Here's the secret most people miss:

**Running count is useless for betting decisions. True count is everything.**

A running count of +10 with 5 decks left is weak.
A running count of +5 with 1 deck left is strong.

The true count normalizes for shoe depth and tells you your **actual edge**.

---

## The Formula

**True Count = Running Count ÷ Decks Remaining**

That's it. One division.

The challenge isn't the math. It's estimating decks remaining while maintaining concentration.

---

## Quick Deck Estimation

### Method 1: Discard Tray

Look at the discard tray (where dealt cards go):

| Discard Tray Level | Decks Dealt | Decks Remaining (6-deck) |
|-------------------|-------------|-------------------------|
| Nearly empty | ~1 | ~5 |
| 1/3 full | ~2 | ~4 |
| Half full | ~3 | ~3 |
| 2/3 full | ~4 | ~2 |
| Nearly full | ~5 | ~1 |

### Method 2: Hands Dealt

Average hand = ~2.7 cards. Track roughly how many hands have been dealt:
- 20 hands dealt ≈ 1 deck
- 40 hands dealt ≈ 2 decks

### Method 3: Shoe Position

Watch where the cut card is and estimate penetration:
- Just started = lots of decks remaining
- Cut card visible = ~1 deck left
- Cut card hit = shoe ends soon

**Pro tip**: Practice estimation before you play. Watch a shoe, guess remaining decks, then check.

---

## True Count to Edge Conversion

This is why counting works:

| True Count | Player Edge | What To Do |
|------------|-------------|------------|
| TC < 0 | House has 1%+ edge | Minimum bets (or leave) |
| TC 0 | House has ~0.5% edge | Minimum bets |
| TC +1 | Break-even | Minimum bets |
| TC +2 | Player has 0.5% edge | Increase bet modestly |
| TC +3 | Player has 1.0% edge | Increase bet more |
| TC +4 | Player has 1.5% edge | Near max bet |
| TC +5+ | Player has 2%+ edge | Max bet |

**Rule of thumb**: Each +1 in true count shifts edge by ~0.5%.

At TC +2, the player has an edge. That's when you start making money.

## Betting Based on True Count

This is the key insight most "card counters" miss:

**It's not about winning individual hands. It's about having more money in play when you have an edge.**

### Bet Spread Basics

| True Count | Suggested Bet (as multiple of min bet) |
|------------|----------------------------------------|
| TC ≤ 0 | 1x (minimum) |
| TC +1 | 1-2x |
| TC +2 | 2-4x |
| TC +3 | 4-6x |
| TC +4 | 6-8x |
| TC +5+ | 8-12x |

Example: $25 minimum table, 1-8 spread
- TC 0: Bet $25
- TC +2: Bet $50-100
- TC +4: Bet $150-200
- TC +5+: Bet $200

## Mental Math Shortcuts

### Rounding

Don't calculate 7 ÷ 2.5 = 2.8. Just round:
- 7 ÷ 2.5 → 7 ÷ 3 = ~2.3 (close enough)

You're making betting decisions, not rocket science. Being in the right ballpark is sufficient.

### Fractional Decks

When less than 1 deck remains:
- Half deck left: RC × 2 = TC
- 3/4 deck left: RC × 1.5 ≈ TC (round up)

Example: RC +4 with half deck remaining → TC = +8 (great spot!)

### Pre-Calculate Benchmarks

Know these conversions cold:

| Running Count | 4 Decks Left | 2 Decks Left | 1 Deck Left |
|---------------|--------------|--------------|-------------|
| +4 | TC +1 | TC +2 | TC +4 |
| +6 | TC +1.5 | TC +3 | TC +6 |
| +8 | TC +2 | TC +4 | TC +8 |
| +10 | TC +2.5 | TC +5 | TC +10 |

## The "Wong" Strategy

Named after Stanford Wong (legendary counter), "Wonging" means only betting when the count is favorable.

### Back-Counting

Stand behind the table and count. Only sit down when TC ≥ +1 or +2.

Advantages:
- Skip all the negative-count hands
- Maximize time spent with an edge
- Extend your bankroll

Disadvantages:
- Not allowed in many casinos
- Very obvious to surveillance
- Can't always find an open seat

### Mid-Shoe Entry

Join tables mid-shoe when you've counted favorable. Many casinos restrict this, especially in high-limit rooms.

### Table Hopping

Play at TC +2 or better, leave when it drops to 0 or below. Move to another table.

More subtle than back-counting, but requires multiple tables with open seats.
