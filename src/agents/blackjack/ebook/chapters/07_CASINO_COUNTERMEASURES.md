# Chapter 7: Casino Countermeasures — How Not to Get Caught

## They're Watching

Casinos aren't stupid. They've known about card counting since Ed Thorp published "Beat the Dealer" in 1962.

They also can't do much about it legally. Counting isn't cheating — you're just using your brain.

But they CAN:
- Back you off (ask you to leave)
- Restrict your play (flat betting only)
- Ban you from the property
- Share your info with other casinos

Here's how to avoid all of that.

---

## What Surveillance Looks For

### 1. Betting Patterns

The biggest tell. If you're betting $25 at low counts and $300 at high counts, they'll notice.

**Red flags:**
- Big jumps from min to max bet
- Bet increases after many small cards dealt
- Bet decreases after many face cards

**Cover:** Don't make giant jumps. Go $25 → $50 → $100 → $200, not $25 → $200 instantly.

### 2. Correlation Between Count and Decisions

Pro counters take insurance at TC +3 but not otherwise. They deviate from basic strategy at specific counts.

Surveillance can track thousands of your decisions and run statistical analysis.

**Cover:** Take insurance randomly sometimes. Make deliberate "wrong" plays occasionally (small bets only).

### 3. Playing Behavior

Counters often:
- Stare intensely at cards
- Don't drink or socialize
- Move lips while counting
- Join mid-shoe and leave mid-shoe
- Play alone

**Cover:** Be a normal recreational player. Chat with the dealer. Order a drink (don't drink much). Take breaks.

### 4. Wonging (Back-Counting)

Standing behind tables, watching, only sitting when count is good — this is extremely obvious.

**Cover:** If you're going to jump in mid-shoe, have a reason. "Decided to try this table" beats standing there for 10 minutes watching.

## Cover Betting Techniques

### Gradual Ramps

Don't spike your bets. Build up over multiple hands:

**Bad:** $25 → $25 → $25 → $200
**Good:** $25 → $25 → $50 → $75 → $150 → $200

### Betting "Reasons"

Create plausible reasons for bet changes:
- "I won last hand, let it ride"
- "Time to press my luck"
- "Cutting back to cool off"

These are cover stories, not strategies.

### Cover Bets

Occasionally make a decent-sized bet at low or neutral counts. It confuses the pattern.

**Cost:** You lose theoretical EV on these bets
**Benefit:** You stay under the radar longer

### Parlay Cover

After winning a big hand at high count, "let it ride" even if count drops. Looks like gambling behavior.

---

## Cover Play Techniques

### Insurance Cover

Take insurance occasionally at low counts. Costs a bit of EV, but if you ONLY take insurance at TC +3, that's a massive tell.

### Surrender Cover

Don't always surrender when you should. Mix it up.

### Deviation Discipline

At low bet sizes, play basic strategy even when count-based deviations would help. Save deviations for when you have big money out.

## Reading the Heat

"Heat" means the casino is watching you closely. Signs:

| Heat Level | Signs |
|------------|-------|
| **Low** | Pit boss glances occasionally |
| **Medium** | Pit boss watches multiple hands, makes a phone call |
| **High** | Pit boss stands directly behind you, talks into earpiece |
| **Critical** | Gets your player's card, types on computer, security nearby |

### What to Do When You Feel Heat

**Low heat:** Keep playing but moderate your bet spread
**Medium heat:** Color up soon, don't push it
**High/Critical:** Leave calmly after current shoe

Never:
- React visibly to observation
- Leave mid-hand
- Run out of the casino
- Act nervous

Just casually finish the shoe, color up, tip the dealer, and walk away like you would anyway.

## Getting Backed Off

If a casino asks you to leave the blackjack tables:

1. **Stay calm.** It's not a crime. You haven't done anything wrong.
2. **Be polite.** Hostile behavior makes things worse.
3. **Ask clarifying questions.** "Can I play other games?" "Am I banned from the property?"
4. **Color up and leave.** Don't argue. Don't threaten lawsuits.
5. **Don't come back** (to that casino, for a while).

In most jurisdictions, casinos can refuse your play for any reason. Fighting it is pointless.

## Longevity Strategies

The goal isn't to win a million in one night. It's to win consistently over years without getting banned everywhere.

### Spread Your Play

Don't play the same casino every day. Rotate through multiple properties.

### Keep Sessions Short

2-3 hours is plenty. Playing 8-hour sessions every day screams professional.

### Use Camouflage

Dress like a tourist. Chat about "vacation." Ask dealer questions like you don't know the game.

The best counters look like clueless tourists who are "getting lucky."

### Consider Team Play

Teams can avoid detection by having:
- Spotters who count but bet small
- Big players who join for high counts only

Complex but effective. Beyond the scope of this book.

## Your Legal Rights

**USA:**
- Card counting is legal
- Casinos are private property and can refuse service
- No arrest, no criminal penalties

**UK/Canada:**
- Also legal
- Similar situation with private property rights

**Elsewhere:** Research local laws, but counting is rarely illegal anywhere.

## Key Takeaways

✅ Casinos watch betting patterns, not your brain
✅ Cover betting = gradual ramps, not sudden spikes
✅ Act like a recreational player, not a robot
✅ Recognize heat and respond appropriately
✅ Getting backed off isn't the end — just move to another casino
✅ Longevity matters more than any single session
