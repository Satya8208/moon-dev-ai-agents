# Chapter 6: Betting Strategy & Bankroll Management

## The Real Secret

Here's what separates profitable counters from wannabes:

**It's not about counting cards. It's about bet sizing.**

You can count perfectly, but if your betting strategy is wrong, you'll still lose money.

This chapter covers:
- How much to bet based on your edge
- How much bankroll you actually need
- How to survive the inevitable losing streaks

---

## The Kelly Criterion

The Kelly Criterion is the mathematically optimal bet sizing formula. It maximizes long-term growth while minimizing ruin risk.

### The Formula

**Kelly Bet = (Edge / Odds) × Bankroll**

For blackjack (simplified):
**Bet = Edge × Bankroll**

Example:
- TC +3 = 1% edge
- Bankroll = $10,000
- Kelly bet = 0.01 × $10,000 = $100

### Full Kelly vs Fractional Kelly

Betting full Kelly is volatile. Most pros use **Half Kelly** or **Quarter Kelly**:

| Kelly % | Bet Size | Volatility | Bankroll Needed |
|---------|----------|------------|-----------------|
| Full (100%) | Maximum growth | Very high | Lower |
| Half (50%) | Good growth | Moderate | Medium |
| Quarter (25%) | Slower growth | Low | Higher |

**Recommendation**: Use Half Kelly. It provides decent growth while cutting risk of ruin dramatically.

## Betting Spreads

A **betting spread** is the ratio between your minimum and maximum bet.

| Spread | Min Bet | Max Bet | When to Use |
|--------|---------|---------|-------------|
| 1-4 | $25 | $100 | Conservative, small bankroll |
| 1-8 | $25 | $200 | Standard, medium bankroll |
| 1-12 | $25 | $300 | Aggressive, large bankroll |
| 1-16+ | $25 | $400+ | Professional level |

### Larger Spreads = More Profit (But More Heat)

Big betting swings attract attention. If you go from $25 to $300 suddenly, the casino notices.

**Balance**: Enough spread to make money, not so much you get backed off.

## How Much Bankroll Do You Need?

This is where most people fail. They're undercapitalized.

### Minimum Bankroll Requirements

| Spread | Bankroll Needed | Example (Min Bet $25) |
|--------|-----------------|----------------------|
| 1-4 | 150 × max bet | $15,000 |
| 1-8 | 200 × max bet | $40,000 |
| 1-12 | 250 × max bet | $75,000 |

These numbers give you ~5% risk of ruin.

### Reality Check

If you're playing a $25 table with a 1-8 spread (max bet $200), you need ~$40,000 bankroll to play "correctly."

**Don't have that?** Options:
1. Play lower stakes ($5 or $10 tables)
2. Accept higher risk of ruin
3. Build bankroll slowly at lower stakes first

---

## Risk of Ruin

Risk of Ruin (ROR) is the probability you'll lose your entire bankroll before doubling it.

| Bankroll in Max Bets | Risk of Ruin |
|---------------------|--------------|
| 100 | ~40% |
| 200 | ~13% |
| 300 | ~5% |
| 400 | ~2% |
| 500 | <1% |

**Translation**: Even with perfect play, if you're undercapitalized, you WILL go broke eventually.

## Session Management

Don't think of blackjack as individual sessions. Think of it as one continuous game.

That said, practical session management helps:

### Win Goals

Some pros set session win goals (e.g., leave when up $500). This can help lock in profits psychologically.

Mathematical reality: It doesn't matter. The long run doesn't care when you stop.

### Loss Limits

More useful for:
- Preserving bankroll when running cold
- Avoiding tilt (emotional play)
- Managing fatigue

A common approach: Leave after losing 30-50% of session buy-in.

### Session Duration

Counting requires concentration. Most people can maintain focus for:
- Beginner: 1-2 hours
- Intermediate: 2-4 hours
- Expert: 4-6 hours

Don't play when tired or after drinking.

## Understanding Variance

Even with an edge, you'll have losing sessions. Many of them.

### Standard Deviation

In blackjack, standard deviation is ~1.15 bet units per hand. Over 1,000 hands:

| Your Edge | Expected Profit | Standard Deviation | Range (95% confidence) |
|-----------|-----------------|-------------------|----------------------|
| 1% | +10 units | ~36 units | -62 to +82 units |
| 0.5% | +5 units | ~36 units | -67 to +77 units |

**Key insight**: Even with a 1% edge over 1,000 hands, you might be down 60+ units. That's just variance.

### What This Means Practically

- You need thousands of hands for edge to show
- Short-term results are nearly random
- Don't judge your play by individual sessions
- Trust the math

| Factor | Conservative | Standard | Aggressive |
|--------|-------------|----------|------------|
| Kelly % | 25% | 50% | 75%+ |

| Expected Hourly (hands/hr) | Lower | Medium | Higher |

## Key Takeaways

✅ Bet sizing matters more than counting accuracy
✅ Use Kelly Criterion (Half Kelly is safest)
✅ Need ~200 × max bet as minimum bankroll
✅ Larger spreads = more profit but more casino attention
✅ Variance is brutal — think long-term
✅ Undercapitalization kills advantage players
